  • Crafting subcategories:
    • Items: mostly boring stuff, like consumables or ingredients for further crafting
    • Components: items that can be assembled into equipment
      Their quality doesn't transfer directly to the equipment; their stats show as "expected values"
      i.e. the stats of equipment made at the corresponding quality.
    • Equipment: things you can actually wear, mostly made of components.
      Quality is affected by skill level and component tier.
  • You get items from passive activities and loot; items usable in crafting usually mention their use in the description.
    For passive activities, the time needed, success chance and item count are all affected by skill level.
  • Quality and quality caps
    • Equipment quality falls within a range and is also adjusted by a quality cap.
      In general, equipment quality correlates negatively with component tier, and positively with workbench tier and skill level.
    • Component quality formula: (80 + skill level * 2 + workbench tier * 10 - component tier * 10 ± 15)% rounded to the nearest multiple of 4% (for easier stacking)
      Equipment quality formula: [weighted average of component quality by (component tier + 1)] ± 15%
    • Note: component tiers and workbench tiers currently range 0~14.
  • Bulk crafting and true bulk crafting
    • Use the [max] button to bulk craft.
      In default bulk mode, every single craft is calculated in full.
      It's effectively the same as clicking the craft button many times.
    • True bulk crafting for items activates when the success rate is 100%.
      It calculates how many can be made, then grants the XP and items in one go.
      Without true bulk crafting, for performance, one click of max crafts at most 1001 times.
    • True bulk crafting for components activates when 1000+ components can be made.
      It splits the batch into 100 rounds (fractional parts handled specially so the total stays the same)
      Quality is distributed evenly within each round, and XP is granted at the end of each round.
      At 1000+ scale, this approximation has no significant effect.
      And this way, even 9e15 materials is no problem!
    • True bulk crafting for equipment activates when every selected component count exceeds 100.
      Quality is split evenly. I really couldn't be bothered to add a standard deviation proportional to √N.
      Likewise, this completely erases the performance problem here!

    • Heat value [1]: one Thunder Shield fully burned releases only 1 [primal energy].
    • It can be used to [fully burn] the two fuels above, but can never be [fully burned] itself.

  • Basics:
    • The [Frigid Phase-Change Engine] is a [device] Neko built herself on the Pure White Arctic Tundra.
    • Its full name would be [Freezing Air-Cooled Second-Order Phase Transformation Engine]
    • The principle is crude and simple... compressing gas releases heat, and expanding gas absorbs heat.
    • So compress the gas outside and let it expand inside an insulated bag, and you have refrigeration!
    • Once Glacial Superfluid is cooled to extremely low temperatures, its ice-element absorption rate grows super-exponentially.
    • Then the [Ten-Thousand-Year Ice Marrow] formation that should take millennia can be compressed into minutes or even seconds.
    • Inspiration: the inverse Arrhenius equation & chapter 1 of physical chemistry.

  • Energy source:
    • Obviously no kind of [Energy Core] can absorb heat...
    • and a simple working-fluid turbine is hopelessly inefficient; metal that withstands tens of thousands of degrees and thousands of TPa at once is rare even at Sky Rank.
    • So! Neko has to push the piston herself!
    • To avoid precision issues, gas amount / piston volume can change by at most e or 1/e per second.
    • If that cap isn't reached, the speed of [Compress] and [Inflate] depends entirely on Neko's ability.
    • Power = ATK ^ 1.5, in W; the display shows GW.
    • Oh, and remember to switch to a single-target arcane art. There aren't several pistons here for you to AoE.

  • Piston - Compress / Expand:
    • [Glacial Air] is a monatomic gas extremely close to an ideal gas.
    • So its adiabatic index is about 5/3, meaning under adiabatic operation the proportional change in pressure is 5/3 that of volume.
    • Anyone who took chemistry knows the remaining 2/3 becomes heat!
    • While the piston is in the environment, its temperature approaches ambient at a rate of 0.1(T2-T1).
    • The default ambient temperature of the frigid tundra is 240K.
    • That represents conduction.The latter represents thermal radiation, which dominates above 1200K.
    • Once most of the excess heat is gone, release and let the gas [Expand].
    • Its temperature then drops, cooling the insulated bag.
    • The expansion limit is 30m³ and the compression limit is 0.3m³.
    • Neko's craftsmanship isn't great; the piston bottom is bumpy and won't go further...
    • Why was thermal radiation removed?
    • There used to be thermal radiation. Sadly, above 12000K it was extremely unstable and broke the simulation with NaNs everywhere.
    • Specifically, within 1 frame it radiated its own temperature down to a huge negative number...
    • A differential equation could give the true rate of change, but that's just too much work.
    • So until Sayuki thinks of something better... we can live without it.

  • Piston - Inflate / Deflate:
    • Want to scale up production? No problem!
    • The piston starts with 100 million mol of Glacial Air, but clearly more fits.
    • More air means harder compression, but higher gas heat capacity.
    • If your insulated bag holds a lot of Glacial Superfluid, adding more Glacial Air is almost mandatory!
    • It releases a little heat, but since cold air mixes in, the temperature barely rises.
    • The tundra's default pressure is 12MPa / 120bar / 118.4308atm.
    • So deflation can only go down to 12MPa.
    • By the way, if anyone's curious, the approximation used in the inflation calculation is:
    • the incoming Glacial Air is adiabatically compressed to the internal pressure, then the whole thing is adiabatically compressed once more, then allowed to reach thermal equilibrium.
    • The result:
    • $$ \frac{dN}{dt} = \frac{P}{RT_1[\frac{25}{6}(\frac{p_2}{p_1})^{\frac{2}{5}}-\frac{5}{2}]} $$
    • $$ dT = \frac{dN}{N}[\frac{5T_1}{3}(\frac{p_2}{p_1})^{\frac{2}{5}}-T_2] $$
    • dN/dt is air accumulation rate (mol/s), N total gas (mol), P power, R the ideal gas constant, T2 internal temperature, p2 internal pressure, T1 external temperature, p1 external pressure, dT temperature change.
    • Remember: bite off more than you can chew...
    • If you pump in far too much air, compression becomes extremely hard and takes ages.
    • Luckily there's the [Deflate] button: reduces the gas in the piston to 1/e per second. Careful not to overdo it!
    • If you need it, you clearly bit off more than you could chew.

  • Glacial Superfluid / heat exchange:
    • An extremely conductive material. Relaxation time under 1 frame [40ms]
    • To minimize heat absorbed from outside, it's always piled into a sphere.
    • When the piston is in the insulated bag, it exchanges heat with the Glacial Superfluid.
    • 1m³ of Glacial Superfluid has the heat capacity of 10,000,000 mol of Glacial Air.
    • Heat exchanged is proportional to the reduced heat capacity Q1Q2/(Q1+Q2) and the temperature difference.
    • Simply put, if one side's heat capacity is tiny, the reduced capacity approaches that side's.
    • So try to keep your Glacial Superfluid and Glacial Air heat capacities matched!
    • Newly added Glacial Superfluid is at ambient temperature.
    • That means adding more to deeply cooled superfluid greatly dilutes the temperature difference.

  • Porous Ice Crystal / insulation:
    • Since conductance is inversely proportional to thickness, extending the Cauchy inequality to infinitely many area elements shows:
    • spreading the insulation evenly over the superfluid's surface is optimal.
    • Insulation thickness is the difference between two sphere radii; 1pt of Porous Ice Crystal = 0.1m³.
    • So adding more Glacial Superfluid both increases surface area and reduces insulation thickness.
    • Instantaneous rate of temperature change = 0.001 * area * (bag - ambient temperature) / volume / insulation thickness.
    • Under initial conditions that's 4.6%/s, or a 14.9s half-life for the heat deficit you create.
    • More insulation never hurts! Unless you compress gas inside the bag...

  • Ice element / Ten-Thousand-Year Ice Marrow:
    • Glacial Superfluid becomes [Ten-Thousand-Year Ice Marrow] once it accumulates over 1000pts/m³ of [ice element].
    • [Ice element] is tiny subatomic-scale fairies. They tell molecules to stop moving.
    • No idea why, but [ice element] accumulation is the exact opposite of normal reaction kinetics.
    • Instead of the usual v=Ae^(-Ep/RT), [ice element] accumulates per unit area at σ=a*e^(b/T).
    • Fitting to 'converts in 100s at 80K' and 'converts in ten thousand years at 240K',
    • we get a=3.4764e-14, b=2623.17.
    • My point is: a small drop in temperature massively speeds up ice element accumulation!
    • So find ways to lower the bag's temperature! Even using less Glacial Superfluid is worth it!
    • Fun fact: this corresponds to an activation energy of -21.80kJ/mol for the ice element capture reaction.
    • Since it can't flow once it becomes [Ten-Thousand-Year Ice Marrow], [ice element] caps at 1000pts/m³.
    • To avoid floating-point error, [Ten-Thousand-Year Ice Marrow] can be extracted once [ice element] exceeds 999.999pts/m³.

    • Equipping [Flame-Sea Frost Sky] (Domain Stage 2/3) unlocks [Flame Sea] and [Frost Sky].
    • [Flame Sea]: 192MPa external pressure, 960K.
    • [Frost Sky]: 6.75MPa external pressure, 180K.
    • In the lore, the pressure changes so much because atmospheric convection is intercepted at low altitude by a wide-area ice element array.
    • So at high temperature the air pools here, while at low temperature it disperses quickly!

  • CheeseTricks:
    • You must wonder why there's a button to remove the insulation.
    • Note that the piston-to-bag heat exchange cap is 5x that with air, and 2.5x even at equal heat capacity.
    • Second, with the same amount of gas, higher temperature means higher internal pressure.
    • Third, the inflation formula is complex, but overall higher internal pressure means slower inflation.
    • You can probably already see how to use these!

  • Pressure lock:
    • It withstands several TPa internally, but the engine's reverse seal is terrible.
    • Whenever internal pressure is below external, it equalizes within the next relaxation time.
    • That's because at internal pressure ≈ 27.885% (-) of external, the inflation formula's denominator becomes a tiny negative number.
    • causing the gas amount to become a hugely negative number within one [relaxation time].
    • Then... heat transfer, deflation, expansion, compression all fail in a chain reaction, producing piles of NaN.

  • Basics:
    • The [Family System] is the panel through which Neko leads the clan after defeating her father [start of zone 4-1].
    • Its main purpose is to net-gain money and influence through sensible investment.
    • Most family info updates once per Xuelo day, so income depends directly on time flow
    • Time flow for Sky awake / Sky asleep / Nimbus awake / Nimbus asleep is 48m / 4h / 4h48m / 24h per second
    • which is 384 / 1920 / 2304 / 11520 Xuelo days per real day.
    • Inspiration: Cultivation Family Simulator.

  • Newborns:
    • You can enter the number of newborns per Xuelo day yourself.

    • Note: there are three soft caps at 10K, 100M and 1T, at ^1.5, ^1.75 and ^2.0 respectively.
    • That number roughly represents multi-level management and all the skimming along the way...
    • Overall though, under one system the final value a child produces is fixed.
    • Mind your balance sheet~ though at steady state spending under half of output is probably optimal?
    • If Neko's wealth can't cover the next day's newborns,
    • the daily newborn count resets to zero, so Neko never goes into debt!

  • Breakthrough / death:
    • Nayaka Clan members don't stay the same forever.
    • They may break through, but they may also die.
    • A breakthrough adds 1 to the new realm and removes 1 from the old; a death only removes 1.
    • Death rate is the reciprocal of lifespan.Apprentice rank100 years,Earth Rank1000 years,Sky Rank10000 years,Nimbus Rank100000 years.
      • Remember a year is 50 Xuelo days.
    • Breakthrough rate falls almost monotonically. Only the first 3 minor realms of a major realm are easier than breaking into that major realm.
    • Breakthrough rate / (breakthrough rate + death rate) gives the realm's [advancement rate].
    • A number in the [-xxd] format means the last breakthrough/death was xx Xuelo days ago.
    • See the appendix below for exact numbers.
    • Finally, to avoid being overthrown, scummy Ancestor Neko won't let clan members exceed her by 2 minor realms.
    • So atSky Rank: Breakthroughclan members can reach at mostNimbus Rank: Stage 2, and so on.

  • Training strategy:
    • Keeping the kids at home doing chores is no way for a seasoned clan head to operate.
    • Only trial by blood and fire makes them true warriors... or gets them killed.
    • [Training strategy] has 5 levels.
    • [1] Regular Work: output x0.4, breakthrough x1, death x1.
    • [2] Clan Trials: output x1.0, breakthrough x3, death x5.
    • [3] Secret Realm Expedition: output x2.0, breakthrough x5, death x15.
    • [4] Battlefield Slaughter: output x5.0, breakthrough x10, death x60.
    • [5] Nine Deaths, One Life: output x20, breakthrough x30, death x300.
    • Higher levels yield more realm output.
    • And breakthroughs to higher realms come faster.
    • Even so, at equilibrium, a higher [training strategy] significantly lowers the overall advancement rate.
    • The trade-off is up to you!

  • Economy / influence:
    • Clan members in training have [output].
    • [Dust Rank: Novice] base output is 0.01C per person per day.
    • Each minor realm above multiplies output by 10^(1/3) (~2.15),
    • and each major realm breakthrough by 10^(4/3) (~21.5).
    • A more aggressive [training strategy] also multiplies output.
    • 【Influence] is Act 4's new currency, obtainable only through the [Family System].
    • It's not useful yet. Trust me, it will be soon (before V3.40)!!
    • 【Influence]'s base rate is 100Z -> 1 influence.
    • The [training strategy] effect is squared, but the headcount effect is square-rooted.
    • After all, few people remember a crowd in matching uniforms...
    • but everyone remembers the powerhouse who fought their way out of a Secret Realm's mountain of corpses!

  • Influence milestones:
    • [1 influence] unlocks Baifang's revenge battle (4-1)
    • [50 influence] unlocks Feng Xinghong (sparring) (4-3)
    • [10~100 influence] the 4-1 merchant sells local items; stock grows with influence
    • [100~1000 influence] the 4-3 merchant sells local items; stock grows with influence

  • RNG approximation:
    • In theory, breakthrough/death counts follow a binomial B(n,p).
    • But direct simulation with large n is very expensive and causes heavy lag.
    • So two algorithms are used: [normal approximation] and [Poisson approximation].
    • Honestly, those formulas were copied. Especially the Poisson one.
    • I just tried to prove it and got a divergent integral. Gave up.
